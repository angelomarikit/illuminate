# Receptionist (Staff) — Step-by-step guide

Use this guide if you work the front desk or clinic floor. After you log in, you land on **POS / Sales**. The left menu only shows pages you can use.

**You can:** appointments, customers, POS, sales proof, client sessions, services, loyalty, QR check-in, expenses, consultations, chat, My Work, account settings.

**You cannot:** dashboard, inventory, HR/payroll, clinic settings.

---

## 0. First login (do this once)

1. Open the clinic web app URL.
2. Sign in with the email and temporary password from HR / Owner.
3. Open **Account settings** (My account).
4. Change your password to something only you know.
5. Confirm the correct **branch** is selected (if your clinic has more than one).

**Tip:** Use the **bell** icon for upcoming bookings and pending website bookings.

---

## 1. Start your shift — My Work

1. Open **My Work**.
2. Tap **Time In** when your shift starts (you can also use the top bar if available).
3. Check your hours and leave credits.
4. To request leave: **Request Leave** → choose type, from/to dates, reason → submit.
5. At the end of your shift, tap **Time Out**.

Leave requests wait for Owner/Admin approval.

---

## 2. Handle bookings — Appointments

### Morning checklist

1. Open **Appointments**.
2. Filter **Pending** (website / app bookings waiting for you).
3. For each request:
   - **Approve** if the slot is free, or  
   - **Decline** if you cannot take it.
4. Choose Approve/Decline only, or with SMS / email if your clinic uses those options.
5. Switch the board to **Day** (or Week) and scan today’s schedule.

### Walk-in or new booking

1. Click **Walk-in** or **New appointment**.
2. Choose client, service, staff, date, and time.
3. Save.
4. When the visit is done, mark it **Complete**. To cancel, pick a reason.

---

## 3. Check clients in — QR Check-in

1. Open **QR Check-in**.
2. Enter the booking code or client name (or scan if you use a scanner).
3. Click **Check In Client**.
4. Or tap **Check in** on a row in today’s Arrival Queue.

---

## 4. Client profiles — Customers

### Add a new client

1. Open **Customers**.
2. Click **Add Client**.
3. Fill required fields: **Full name**, **Phone**, **Email**.
4. Optional: birthday, membership (VIP/VVIP may need an expiry date).
5. Save.

### Open an existing client

1. Search by name, phone, or email.
2. Click the row to open the profile panel.
3. Review membership, wallet, points, treatment sessions, visits.
4. Upload a **PDF consent form** when the client signs one (clinic staff only).
5. Use care / doctor notes as needed for the visit.

---

## 5. Sell a service — POS / Sales

1. Open **POS / Sales**.
2. Search and select the **client**.
3. Add services from the list (or **Custom** for a one-off name + price).
4. Set quantity; for packages, fill sessions / package price / next session if shown.
5. Assign staff: Administered by, Consult by, Sales by (when required).
6. Apply discount, loyalty **points**, or **wallet** if the client wants.
7. For GCash / bank / card: optionally **Upload payment screenshot**.
8. Click **Complete sale**.
9. Confirm the sale under **Sales Proof** if you need the receipt or proof image.

---

## 6. Package sessions — Client Sessions

1. Open **Client Sessions**.
2. Filter **Active** packages.
3. Open the client’s package.
4. After the treatment, click **Use 1 session**.
5. Update next session date and notes if needed.
6. When sessions are used up, the package completes automatically.

---

## 7. Wallet & points — Loyalty & Points

### Confirm a cash-in (wallet top-up)

1. First check **Chat Support** for a Cash-in thread with a receipt photo.
2. Mark the chat **Received** only if the money is real.
3. Open **Loyalty & Points**.
4. Choose the client → enter the **amount** → save the cash-in.
5. The client gets a top-up notification on mobile / portal.

### Adjust points

1. Same page → **Adjust Points**.
2. Choose client, enter +/− points and a short note → save.

---

## 8. Client messages — Chat Support

1. Open **Chat Support**.
2. Use filters: **All / Cash-in / Support / Message**.
3. Open a thread.
4. Set **Category** and **Priority** if needed.
5. For cash-in: tap **Received** or **Not received**.
6. Type your reply → Send.
7. When the issue is done, **Close chat**.

---

## 9. Other daily tools

| Page | What to do |
|------|------------|
| **Services** | Check prices and active treatments (edit only if your clinic allows reception to maintain the menu). |
| **AI Consultations** | Save before/after notes or consultation media when used at your clinic. |
| **Expenses** | **Log Expense** → category, amount, description, date, paid by → save. |
| **Sales Proof** | Find past sales, open payment proof, export if needed. |

---

## 10. Suggested daily flow

1. **Time In** (My Work).  
2. Clear **Pending** appointments.  
3. Watch the day’s board + **QR Check-in**.  
4. Check **Chat Support** (especially Cash-in).  
5. Run **POS** and update **Client Sessions** after treatments.  
6. Top up wallets in **Loyalty** only after cash-in is confirmed.  
7. **Time Out** when your shift ends.

---

## Quick troubleshooting

| Problem | What to try |
|---------|-------------|
| Cannot see Inventory or Payroll | Normal — those are not for Receptionist. Ask Owner/Admin. |
| Client not found in POS | Add them under **Customers** first (email helps portal login later). |
| Wallet still empty after cash-in chat | Mark Received in Chat, then record the amount in **Loyalty**. |
| Sale missing | Check **Sales Proof** and the selected branch. |
| Forgot password | Ask HR / Owner to help reset, then change it in Account settings. |
